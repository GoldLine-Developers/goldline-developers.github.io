Tiltix

Tap. Stack. Climb.

A tap-to-drop block stacking game. Land every block, keep your tower standing and climb past the world's famous landmarks.

Get it on Google Play Free to play. No account needed to play.

How it plays

  1. Tap to dropA block slides across the top of your tower. Tap at the right moment to drop it.
  2. Stack it exactlyAnything that hangs over is cut off, so your block gets smaller. Miss completely and the run ends.
  3. Land perfectLand almost exactly on top to keep the full size, earn bonus points and light up your tower.
  4. Keep climbingRise through the zones and pass landmarks. Repair a shrinking block to keep going longer.
