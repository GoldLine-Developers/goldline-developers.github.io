Privacy Policy

Last updated: [August 22, 2026]

CountLine ("the App") is developed by GoldLine Developers ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CountLine does not require an account, login, or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal identity information. All counters, tallies, targets, and history you create are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us or stored on any server we control. We have no backend, no cloud sync, and no access to your counter data.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob and Mediation Partners)
CountLine displays ads through Google AdMob, which may use mediation partners including Meta Audience Network and Unity Ads. These services may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, OS version)
  • General location (approximate, derived from IP address)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions
You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android).

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

In-App Purchases
The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed through Google Play's billing system. We do not receive or store your payment information — this is handled entirely by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, stored locally on your device to unlock the ad-free experience.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration — for haptic feedback on counter targets and completions
  • Network access — required to load ads and process in-app purchases
  • Storage (optional) — only if you use the CSV export feature, to save exported files to your device

4. Children's Privacy

CountLine is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App via a third-party service, please contact us so we can address it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all counter data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. We retain no copy of this data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at getgoldline@gmail.com.
